# Clouds and the Epoch (work/clouds), round 4 (form)

**Artist statement.** The sea of clouds is one cumulus deck seen from above at golden hour: tiny thin pink rows lost in haze at the horizon, flat drifts of thin cloud across the middle deck with their tops warmed toward the sun, and three big near masses, each its own form (a tall tower with wide shoulders at left, nearest the sun; a long flat-topped bank under the island, its right end in the island's broad soft shadow; a low rolling one at right), lit on their upper-left faces in thick cream turning lemon-pink nearest the sun, cool violet on their right faces and bellies with warm reflected light low down, the turning edges melted with the badger, deep violet valleys between them, and the nearest band bluer and softer as if below us. The Epoch flies in front of the left mass as a solid machine painted in planes after the clouds dried: a cream-gold teardrop hull as a cylinder, a bubble canopy of dark glass with the sky in it and one glint, swept wings with thickness, a fin.

## Self-assessment
Works: the recession (tiny far rows, flat drifts, big near masses, blue soft near band); the left mass reads as one big lit form with a tower crown and a wide shoulder; the colour key (cream lights, greyed violet shadows, pinker toward the horizon); the Epoch now reads as a solid machine with a cylindrical hull, a glass dome with a glint, lit far wing and shadowed near wing.
Round 4: the tower reads as one big form with a light side and a shadow side; the bank reads as a different, flat shape; the middle row reads as drifts. Still: the tower's lower rim shows a faint circular edge below the belly, its lit cap is a little marbled, the near-band shoulders are faint, and the bank's top edge is still soft at 1x. Round 3: the deck now sits lighter than the sky's horizon band only on the lit tops; the bellies read violet-grey; the dark blotches are gone. Still gives it away: the centre and right masses are low in contrast and read as soft mauve lumps rather than a lit bank and a rolling form (the island's shadow over the centre flattens it; the lit caps there are pinkish-grey); a few dark blotchy patches in the centre belly and the right valley; the floor is still visibly made of flat horizontal strokes; the lit caps are marbled by the broken-colour mixes in places; the Epoch's hull top is a little yellow and its near-wing underside strip barely reads.
Edges with other areas: everything above y 985 belongs to the sky/island painters (a few far-row strokes reach y 990); the main island's underside (down to y about 1290 at x 1320) is repainted by the island painter over my centre mass; the island's cast shadow on the deck is a cool tint at x 1250-2000, y 1150-1450.
