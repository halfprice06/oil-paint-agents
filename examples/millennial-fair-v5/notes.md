# The Gate Opens at the Millennial Fair (v5)

**Artist statement.** Leene Square in low gold light: bell tower, striped tents, balloons and bunting, a crowd of fairgoers casting long violet shadows, and the time gate between Lucca's telepods pulling Marle into the air while Crono runs toward her.

Engine v5 (soft badger blender, feathered stroke ends). 2400x1600, 27,091 strokes. `final_2x.jpg` is the 4800x3200 render.

**How it was made.** Three painter agents:
- The scene (sky, buildings, ground, gate) was painted from a lit SVG reference the painter drew.
- The crowd and the three main characters were painted by looking at a lit 3D render of posed mannequins (`reference_3d.jpg`, made with `scripts/pose3d`). Joint positions were measured from the render; no pixels were sampled or traced.

**Self-assessment.** Painting from a real lit reference fixed the main failure of earlier versions (people as pegs and dolls): the crowd has weight, varied poses and cast shadows, and Crono and Marle have believable gestures. Still weak: figures are smooth and a little clay-like up close, faces are minimal, cloth has few folds, Lucca's body is stiff with blob boots, and the tower and clouds are crude.
