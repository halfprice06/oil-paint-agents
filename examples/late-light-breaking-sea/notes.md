# Late Light, Breaking Sea

**Artist statement.** One wave breaks under a heavy late-afternoon overcast: the curl is highest and clearest at left-centre with green light through the lip and a billowing burst of thrown foam, while the shoulder runs on lower, smooth and unbroken. A dark headland anchors the left, a soft band of warm light sits low on the right with a broken light path on the sea beneath it, and a wet beach mirrors the sky and the foam. The design is a few large masses; value and edge decisions follow the light gap.

## Method
SVG colour study (study_color.svg), then passes: drawing, sky, sea, blend (sand only), wave face, sand, foam, sky masses and horizon glow, headland, accents. Round 3 changes: the sea is painted in broken strokes (dark trough under lighter sky-reflecting top, tilted and curved, smaller and flatter toward the horizon) with the blender used only on the beach and a short stretch of horizon; the thrown foam is built from rounded lobes (cool grey-blue shadow side, warm thick lit crown, low-load dissolving rim); base foam is irregular patches with holes of dark water, shadow foam in front of lit foam; the headland has an irregular silhouette, broad warm-grey top planes, cool grey-violet faces and a few dark crevices; the glow is a wide band with a brighter core blended up into the sky; the beach has patches, vertical reflections, damp dark areas and a broken foam edge. Earlier rounds are in src/round1 and src/round2.

## Self-assessment (honest)
Works: wave design and sky masses hold; the sea now shows strokes that recede toward the horizon; the foam burst and base churn read as foam rather than bars; value design reads at thumbnail size.
Still gives it away: the sea strokes are still a little pill-shaped and evenly spaced; the foam burst at left is a vertical stack with a faint ghostly column below it; the headland's ochre top ribbon and diagonal faces look patterned; foam scraps on the sand look like white leaves; reflections on the sand read as curtains; the horizon glow is still a flat strip; paint thickness is uniform across the canvas and there is no knife work or true glazing.

3x check: at full resolution the headland's grey-violet faces read as separate plank-like sticks, the spray flecks look like rice grains, and the ochre top ribbon is a tidy stripe. These are the weakest areas.
