# The Gate Over Guardia Meadow, Late Afternoon

**Artist statement.** A late-afternoon meadow in the Guardia countryside, painted as broken colour in about 12,300 small and large strokes. A red-haired young swordsman, seen from behind at lower left, looks toward a Time Gate that swirls just under the horizon and spills violet-blue light on the grass. A soft castle and a few Fair balloons float in the golden haze. The painting is warm cream, gold and yellow-green light against violet and blue shadow, with the gate as the one cold luminous note.

## Round 3 (dense refinement)
Total 12,308 strokes in 10 passes. Passes 01-05 are the block-in and are unchanged from round 2 except that the foreground flicks and dot flowers were removed from 05. Backups are in src/round1 and src/round2.
- 06 sky: about 4,000 strokes. Clouds are built from five layers of overlapping strokes with lit tops, rose halftones and violet undersides. The blue sky is a separate set of strokes, and there is a horizon glow.
- 07 meadow: about 4,400 strokes. Strokes get smaller toward the horizon, with dappled light and cloud shadows, flicks, glints, gate light spilled on the grass and wildflower clusters.
- 08 gate: about 1,400 strokes. A scumbled halo, spiral arms, broken segments, streamers, a warm core and a few sparks.
- 09 distance: about 570 strokes. Ridge, trees, a soft castle and tiny balloons.
- 10 figure: about 900 strokes. A young man about 195px tall, with flame hair, a tunic with cream light and violet shade, a flowing teal scarf, dark trousers, a katana and a glint.

## Self-assessment (honest)
Works: the sky now has real cloud masses and a soft horizon glow; the meadow reads as dappled impressionist grass with coloured shadows and clusters of flowers; the gate reads as a luminous vortex; the figure reads at 1x as a young man in light, and at 2x he holds up better than the earlier sprite.

Still gives it away:
- The meadow is busy and even in scale. It lacks big simple forms such as a rolling hill, and there are muddy ochre-brown dabs in the left foreground.
- The gate's halo is a heavy purple fog.
- The castle is a row of rounded pill towers, and a thin cyan line shows at the ridge.
- Clouds are soft and blurry with little crisp edge.
- The figure's tunic shadow side is a little striped, the scarf is slightly stiff and too cyan, and the hair has a few stray yellow dabs.
- Stroke ends are still rounded. Many small strokes of similar size read as texture, not as deliberate marks.
- I judged the figure at 2x on one crop and the gate at 2x only before its last fixes. The 3x render was not inspected.
