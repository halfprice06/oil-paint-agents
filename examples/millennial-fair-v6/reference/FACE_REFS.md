# Face and hand references (Millennial Fair v6)

Real photographs for the micro detail the mannequins lack: eye sockets, lids, nose, lips, cheekbones, jaw, ears,
hair, and hands with fingers. Each one is chosen to match the angle or the light of a figure in the scene.
**LOOK at them. Do not copy or sample them.** Take the planes and the way light turns across them, then paint the
character's own features at the angle and in the light of `../master.png`.

Every photo was found through the Openverse API (api.openverse.org) and comes from Flickr. Each is under a
Creative Commons licence that allows commercial use, or is marked public domain. They are kept here only as
private study references, not reproduced in the painting. Credit is below. Two of them are CC BY-ND (no
derivatives). That is fine for looking, but do not edit or redistribute those two files.

### Which photo for which figure
- **Marle:**
  - `photo_marle_01`: face tipped up in warm low side light. The closest match for her face, throat and jaw.
  - `photo_marle_02`: back-lit red hair. Use it for the rim.
  - `photo_marle_03` and `photo_marle_04`: strawberry-blonde and red-haired three-quarter faces. Use them for colour and features.
  - `photo_marle_05` and `photo_marle_06`: ponytails.
- **Lucca:** `photo_lucca_01` to `03`: glasses on young faces in three-quarter. Note how the frames sit over the eye sockets and catch light. `photo_lucca_02` also shows a covered head, like her helmet.
- **Crono:**
  - `photo_crono_01` to `03`: young profiles in warm side light against dark. Use them for cheek, ear and jaw.
  - `photo_crono_04`: a runner's head in profile.
  - `photo_crono_05` and `photo_crono_06`: sprinting stride and fists.
- **Fairgoers:**
  - `photo_fairgoer_01`: a boy pointing.
  - `photo_fairgoer_02`: a girl pointing up.
  - `photo_fairgoer_03`: a crowd in sunset light.
  - `photo_fairgoer_04`: an elderly face in warm sun.
- **Hands:**
  - `photo_hand_01` and `photo_hand_02`: reaching hands with spread fingers in warm light and back light. Use them for Marle's outstretched right hand.
  - `photo_hand_03`: an open hand in side light.
  - `photo_hand_04`: raised hands against the sky. Use it for Lucca's raised arm and for shielding.
  - `photo_hand_05`: cheering hands.
