# The Gate Opens at the Millennial Fair (v6 continuation: everything except the three heroes)

## Artist statement
A low gold sun from the left rakes across a plaza of worn flagstones and a weathered stage; the gate, a funnel of dark indigo with spiral arms of glazed violet and thick pale light and a hot white core, throws cold light back onto the tree, the deck and the pods. Warm against cool, thick lights against thin dark glazes. The three heroes are NOT painted here (heroes-dev supplies 30_crono, 31_lucca, 32_marle); the area behind and around them is fully painted so those passes go on top. painting.json lists no hero passes.

## Round 3 (coordinator critique, worst first)
1. Steps: 29d now paints two real steps: lit tread (pink-brown, lilac on the right from the gate), bright broken nosing, dark nosing line and its shadow on the riser, ONE broad riser per step in a warm brown graded from shadow to floor bounce (wet-blended so it is not a stack of bars), faint grain, cast shadows onto the paving and the lower tread. 
2. Deck: 29b is a full repaint. Boards run to the vanishing point (1370,830), 60 px at the front edge; each plank is laid in horizontal rows so it is one smooth lit plane, then gap line, sun bevel, grain and butt joints are drawn along the rays. Light is built into the colour: warm sun stripes, lilac cast shadows of the left pod and the console, the gate's cold light pooling at the middle of the back edge. Lesson: a flat brush at an arbitrary slant gave spikes; rows plus round-brush edge lines worked.
3. Paving: 29c is a full repaint with a new layout (own PRNG): varied widths (cobbles to long slabs), jittered and skewed corners, rounded outlines, per-stone tone from warm pink-buff on the left to lilac-grey on the right, lit worn left/back edge, shadowed right/front edge, pits, lichen, cracks. 29i adds a second skin of broken colour and grit. Cast shadows are broad violet glazes.
4. Console: 29h rebuilt from planes (left face graded pale lilac to indigo with wet blending and a lit edge, near-black enamel front with a violet sheen and floor bounce, brown lid with a gold chamfer, thick front edge, lever shadows).
5. Far houses simplified (03b: plaster plane, roof courses, clean windows); sky: streak strokes cut back (01, 28) so it is a calm warm gradient with a few soft clouds.
6. Dome leaf dabs removed (exclusion in 28); gate flecks reduced and recoloured toward lilac-white, new luminous broken rim crescent and bloom (29_gate_refine section 8).

## Honest self-assessment (after round 3)
Better: deck is now readable planks in perspective with light and shadow; steps are two believable wooden steps; sky is calm; paving is a continuous irregular stone field; the gate rim glows.
Still short of a Sorolla at 2x: scene strokes are ~31k, not 40k. Paving stones are still wide horizontal pillows with stair-stepped ends and the joints are a little too even; the deck is a bit uniform red-brown and has a streaky band at the front edge; the houses are hazy (gate glow and glazes veil them) and the right house roof is hidden; the console left face shows horizontal row marks; the dark band under the stage skirt is heavier than the master; foliage was not touched this round and is still the 'leaf stamp' look at close range.
